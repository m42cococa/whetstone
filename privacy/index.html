Whetstone Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · applies to the Whetstone: Mental Math app for iPhone and iPad

In short: Whetstone does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. Everything the app does runs on your device. The developer has no server and receives no data from the app.

Data we collect

None. Whetstone does not collect any information that identifies you, your device or how you use the app. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting libraries, and it makes no network requests of its own. In the App Store's privacy section the app is listed as Data Not Collected.

Data on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things on your device, in its own storage, where other apps cannot read them:

WhatWhy
Your progress: best stars and times per level, and per day and skill how many problems you solved or missed, how many on the first try, and how long they tookTo show your stars, streak, edges and charts, and to choose today's workout
Recently missed problem types (for example "division with remainder, level 1-3")To bring them back in the Daily Workout
The level or workout in progressTo continue where you left it
Settings: sounds, haptics, reminder timeTo remember your choices
Whether Whetstone Full was purchased (a copy of what the App Store reports)To open the right levels when the app starts
A random install identifierTo keep each device's progress apart when syncing through iCloud; it contains nothing about you or your device

This data is included in your device backups, according to your backup settings; the install identifier is excluded from them.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Whetstone stores your progress in your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it is the same on your iPhone and iPad. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see or access it. The level in progress and your settings are not synced.

To turn sync off, disable iCloud for Whetstone in your device's iCloud settings. Progress then stays on each device.

The daily reminder

The reminder is off until you turn it on in Settings; only then does the app ask for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Whetstone Full is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer receives no payment details and no information about who you are. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock the levels. Purchase and refund records are kept by Apple under its privacy policy.

Permissions

Whetstone asks for permission to show notifications only if you turn on the daily reminder. It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

Diagnostics shared by Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects and anonymizes these under its own policy; Whetstone itself sends nothing. You can turn this off at any time in the same settings.

Deleting your data

  • Progress: Settings → Delete all progress. This clears it on all your devices.
  • Everything on a device: delete the app.
  • iCloud: turn off iCloud for Whetstone in your device's iCloud settings. Data in your iCloud account is managed by Apple and can be removed through your Apple Account's iCloud settings.

Children

Whetstone is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Union, United Kingdom) and the CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data and to object to its processing. Because the developer collects and holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or share. The data the app keeps is on your devices and in your iCloud account, where you control it as described above. Whetstone does not sell or share personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page and the App Store privacy section will say so before the update is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/m42cococa/whetstone/issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.
